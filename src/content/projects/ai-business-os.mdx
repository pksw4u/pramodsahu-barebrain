## Overview

An AI-powered business operating system built with LangGraph, LangChain, and
DeepAgents, exploring agent-based orchestration for coordinating multiple business
processes and operational workflows. The project is currently under development.

## Problem

Business processes across an organisation run as separate, manual threads. Coordinating
them requires a human in the middle; there is no single system that can reason across
operations and act.

## What I Built

An agent-based orchestration layer that coordinates multiple business processes and
operational workflows, currently in development.
