## Overview

An AI-powered receptionist capable of handling inbound calls, understanding customer
enquiries, and connecting conversations with automated business workflows and external
tools.

## Problem

Inbound calls needed a first point of contact that could understand the enquiry and
route it correctly, without a human answering every call.

## What I Built

An AI receptionist that handles inbound calls, understands customer enquiries, and
connects the conversation to automated business workflows and external tools.

<div class="diagram">

```text
inbound call -> voice AI (Retell/Vapi) -> intent -> n8n -> business workflow / tools
```

</div>

## Technology

n8n for orchestration, Retell/Vapi for voice AI, and APIs for external tools.

## Outcome

Designed to provide a practical, automated first point of contact. This case study
documents the architecture rather than results; no client metrics are published here.
