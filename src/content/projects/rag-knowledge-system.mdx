## Overview

A RAG workflow built in n8n to enable AI responses grounded in business-specific
information, so answers reflect the company's own knowledge rather than generic model
output.

## Problem

AI responses about the business were ungrounded and unreliable. Customers and staff
needed answers drawn from the company's actual information, not plausible guesses.

## What I Built

An n8n workflow that retrieves relevant business knowledge and feeds it into the AI
response, keeping answers grounded in the source material.

<div class="diagram">

```text
question -> retrieval over business knowledge -> grounded context -> LLM -> answer
```

</div>

## Technology

n8n for orchestration, RAG for grounded responses, and an LLM for generation.

## Outcome

Designed to provide context-aware responses based on business knowledge. This case
study documents the architecture rather than results; no client metrics are published
here.
