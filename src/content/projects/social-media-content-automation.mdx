## Overview

An automated content-generation workflow in n8n for creating social media content,
connecting AI generation with workflow automation to streamline recurring
content-production tasks.

## Problem

Social media content was produced by hand on a recurring basis. The same kind of post
was written and scheduled repeatedly, taking time away from higher-value work.

## What I Built

An n8n workflow that generates social media content with AI and routes it through the
publishing steps automatically.

<div class="diagram">

```text
trigger -> n8n -> AI generation -> content -> publishing step
```

</div>

## Technology

n8n for orchestration and AI generation for content.

## Outcome

Designed to streamline recurring content production. This case study documents the
architecture rather than results; no client metrics are published here.
