## Overview

A document-processing workflow combining OCR, PDF parsing, AI extraction, and
structured data processing.

## Problem

Invoice data required repeatable extraction from unstructured documents. The same
information arrived in a different layout on every supplier's invoice, so the work
was manual, slow, and inconsistent.

## What I Built

A document pipeline where documents are received, parsed, extracted into structured
fields, and passed through validation.

<div class="diagram">

```text
document -> OCR -> PDF parsing -> AI extraction -> validation -> structured fields
```

</div>

Two choices are worth naming. Extraction runs against local LLMs, which keeps
invoice contents off third-party APIs. And validation sits after extraction rather
than being folded into the prompt — a field that fails its check should be visible as
a failure, not silently absent.

## Technology

OCR for text recovery, PDF parsing for layout, local LLMs for extraction, and
structured data mapping for the output shape.

## Outcome

Designed as a foundation for dependable document processing: the pipeline is
repeatable across invoice layouts rather than tuned to one. No client metrics are
published for this project.
