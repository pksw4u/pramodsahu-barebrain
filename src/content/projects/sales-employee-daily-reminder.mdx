## Overview

A daily reminder automation built in Make.com to help sales employees stay on top of their
scheduled activities and follow-ups.

## Problem

Sales activities and follow-ups depended on people remembering. Scheduled calls and tasks
slipped when nobody nudged them, and the pipeline stalled on memory rather than on work.

## What I Built

A Make.com workflow that sends daily reminders to sales employees covering their scheduled
activities and pending follow-ups.

<div class="diagram">

```text
schedule -> Make.com -> daily reminder -> sales employee
```

</div>

## Technology

Make.com for scheduling and delivery.

## Outcome

Designed to create a practical, repeatable reminder workflow. This case study documents the
architecture rather than results; no client metrics are published here.
