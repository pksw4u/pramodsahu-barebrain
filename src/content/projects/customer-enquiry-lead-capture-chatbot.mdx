## Overview

A chatbot for handling inbound customer enquiries and automatically capturing lead
information, connected to Make.com to automate lead-processing workflows and downstream
actions.

## Problem

Inbound enquiries arrived as conversation. Lead details were captured inconsistently,
retyped, or lost before they reached anyone who could act on them.

## What I Built

A chatbot that responds to customer enquiries, captures structured lead details, and
passes them into Make.com for downstream processing.

<div class="diagram">

```text
enquiry -> chatbot -> structured lead details -> Make.com -> lead processing
```

</div>

## Technology

Make.com for orchestration and lead processing.

## Outcome

Designed to create a practical, repeatable enquiry-to-lead workflow. This case study
documents the architecture rather than results; no client metrics are published here.
