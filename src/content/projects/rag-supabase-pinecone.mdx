## Overview

Retrieval-augmented AI systems built using vector databases, reranking, and local
language models to provide context-aware responses based on business knowledge.

## Problem

AI responses needed to be grounded in business-specific knowledge, with retrieval and
reranking tuned to the actual question rather than generic model output.

## What I Built

A RAG pipeline that stores knowledge in vector databases, retrieves and reranks
relevant passages, and generates responses with local language models.

<div class="diagram">

```text
question -> vector retrieval (Supabase / Pinecone) -> reranking -> local LLM -> answer
```

</div>

## Technology

n8n for orchestration, Supabase and Pinecone for vector storage, reranking for
relevance, and local LLMs for generation.

## Outcome

Designed to provide context-aware responses based on business knowledge. This case
study documents the architecture rather than results; no client metrics are published
here.
