## Overview

An automated lead-generation and enrichment workflow connecting data collection,
external APIs, AI processing, and downstream business workflows.

## Problem

Lead research and enrichment involved multiple disconnected tools. Data moved by hand
between them, so the pipeline stalled at every step where somebody had to remember to
continue it.

## What I Built

An automation workflow that coordinates collection, enrichment, AI processing, and
downstream routing.

<div class="diagram">

```text
collect -> enrich (external APIs) -> AI processing -> downstream routing
```

</div>

Orchestration is the whole design. Each stage is a service with a clear contract, and
the workflow holds the sequence — which is what turns a checklist of tools into
something that runs without supervision.

## Technology

n8n and Make.com for orchestration, external APIs for enrichment, and AI processing
steps for the data that needs interpreting rather than copying.

## Outcome

Built to connect lead operations into a more systematic workflow. This case study
documents the architecture; no client metrics are published.
