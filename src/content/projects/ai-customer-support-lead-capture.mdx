## Overview

A customer-facing AI assistant for handling inbound enquiries, wired into lead
capture so structured details flow onward automatically.

## Problem

Incoming enquiries and the lead information inside them needed a more consistent
handoff. The information existed, but it arrived as conversation and left as
conversation — retyped, summarised inconsistently, or lost.

## What I Built

An assistant that responds using business context, captures intent, and passes
structured details into n8n.

<div class="diagram">

```text
enquiry -> assistant (RAG over business context)
        -> intent + structured details
        -> n8n  ->  lead routing
```

</div>

The RAG layer is what keeps the assistant's answers grounded in the business rather
than plausible. Once intent and details are structured, the rest is workflow: n8n
takes over and the lead reaches the right place without anyone retyping it.

## Technology

n8n for orchestration, RAG for grounded responses, and an LLM for conversation.

## Outcome

Designed to create a practical, repeatable enquiry-to-lead workflow. This case study
documents the architecture rather than results; no client metrics are published here.
